### Lessons lerned
Through this lab, I gained hands-on experience using a Help Desk ticketing system to simulate real-world IT support workflows. I learned how to create, manage, prioritize, and close support tickets while documenting issues clearly and professionally.

I also practiced communicating solutions, leaving detailed internal notes for other technicians, and properly escalating tickets when an issue could not be resolved at the Tier 1 level. Overall, this lab reinforced the importance of troubleshooting, documentation, and teamwork in an IT support environment.
